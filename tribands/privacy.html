Privacy Policy

TriBands: Triangle Strategy · AybToNext
Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game TriBands: Triangle Strategy ("the App"), developed by AybToNext ("we", "us"), handles information when you use it.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or other personal information directly.

Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • information about ad views and interactions.

This information is processed by Google according to its own policies. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Your choices and consent

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy option in the App's settings.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

4. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Data security and retention

We do not store your personal data on our servers. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at aybtonext@gmail.com.
