Privacy Policy

TriBands: Triangle Board Game · AybToNext
Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game TriBands: Triangle Board Game ("the App"), developed by AybToNext ("we", "us"), handles information when you use it.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or other personal information directly.

Your game progress and settings (player name, chips, level, statistics, markers and preferences) are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this game data in your device backup, which Google stores for you. You can manage backup in your device settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • information about ad views and interactions.

This information is processed by Google according to its own policies. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Chip packs and TriBands Plus are bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see your card or other payment details. The App only receives purchase details (product, order status and a purchase token) to deliver the item and to restore TriBands Plus on a new install. Chips and other in-game items have no real-money value and cannot be exchanged for money.

4. Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

If you allow notifications, the App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (a Google service), which assigns a Firebase installation ID and a notification token so we can send news and reward messages. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

5. Your choices and consent

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices in the App.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • TriBands Plus removes the banner and the full-screen ads shown on launch and between games (rewarded videos stay optional).

6. Deleting your data

Uninstall the App (or clear its storage in your device settings) to delete the data stored on your device. Information handled by Google services is kept according to Google's policies and can be managed in your Google account.

7. Your rights

We do not sell your personal information. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to its use. Because we keep no data on our own servers, most of this can be done with the choices above; for anything else, contact us.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and contains simulated gambling (stakes of in-game chips). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Data security and retention

We do not store your personal data on our servers. Information exchanged with Google services is sent over encrypted connections. Data processed by Google AdMob, Google Play and Firebase is retained according to Google's policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if online play or accounts are added. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at aybtonext@gmail.com.
