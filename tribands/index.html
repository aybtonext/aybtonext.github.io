How to play

  1. On your turn, stretch one band in a straight line over 4 pegs on the hex board.
  2. When your band closes the last open side of a small triangle, that triangle is yours.
  3. One band can close several triangles at once, and every band you place can hand a triangle to your opponent.
  4. Reach your goal first, or hold the most triangles when no band fits.

Chips in TriBands are virtual game currency only: they have no real-world value and cannot be exchanged for money or prizes.
